The best time to trim most trees in Arkansas is late winter to early spring (February through March), while trees are still dormant. Pruning during dormancy reduces stress on the tree, minimizes sap loss, and lowers the risk of pest and disease exposure. Avoid heavy trimming in summer when heat stress can weaken the tree. However, dead or hazardous branches should be removed any time of year.
Most tree removals take between 2 and 6 hours depending on the size of the tree, its location, and accessibility. A small tree in an open yard can be down and cleaned up in under 2 hours.
