Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about tree service in Jonesboro, AR and the surrounding areas.

How much does tree removal cost in Jonesboro, AR?

Tree removal in Jonesboro typically costs between $500 and $2,500 depending on the tree's size, location, and condition. Small trees under 30 feet generally cost $500 to $800, medium trees (30-60 feet) run $800 to $1,500, and large trees over 60 feet can range from $1,500 to $2,500 or more. We provide free on-site estimates so you know the exact cost before any work begins.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Jonesboro Tree Pros is fully licensed and insured in the state of Arkansas. We carry comprehensive general liability insurance and full workers' compensation coverage, which protects both your property and our crew. Our team includes ISA-certified arborists who maintain ongoing professional education and training.

Do you offer emergency tree service?

Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency tree service throughout Jonesboro and Craighead County. Whether a storm has knocked a tree onto your home, a large limb is blocking your driveway, or a damaged tree poses an immediate safety risk, our emergency crew can respond quickly to secure the situation and remove the hazard.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Jonesboro and the greater Craighead County area, including Paragould, Batesville, Searcy, West Memphis, Forrest City, Trumann, Brookland, and Lake City. If you are located within our service area and need tree care, call us at (870) 555-0147 for a free estimate.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Jonesboro?

In most cases, a permit is not required to remove a tree on private residential property in Jonesboro. However, certain situations such as trees in protected areas, heritage trees, or trees near public right-of-ways may require approval. Our team will advise you during the estimate process and handle any necessary permitting on your behalf.

How long does tree removal take?

Most single-tree removals are completed in one day, typically within 2 to 6 hours depending on the size and complexity of the job. Large trees near structures or power lines may take a full day. Multi-tree removals or land clearing projects can span two or more days. We will provide a clear timeline during your estimate.

What is the best time of year to trim trees in Arkansas?

The ideal time to trim most trees in Arkansas is during late winter or early spring while the trees are still dormant, typically between January and March. This minimizes stress on the tree and reduces the risk of disease transmission. However, dead, damaged, or hazardous branches can and should be removed at any time of year for safety.

Do you clean up after the job?

Yes, complete cleanup is included in every job we perform. Our crew removes all branches, logs, wood chips, and debris from your property. We rake the work area and leave your yard looking as good or better than when we arrived. If you would like to keep the firewood or wood chips, just let us know and we will stack or spread them wherever you prefer.

How much does stump grinding cost?

Stump grinding in Jonesboro typically costs between $150 and $400 per stump, depending on the stump diameter and accessibility. We grind stumps 6 to 12 inches below the soil surface and backfill the hole with the resulting wood chips. We offer discounts when grinding multiple stumps during the same visit.

Can you save a diseased tree?

In many cases, yes. If caught early enough, many tree diseases can be treated and managed to preserve the tree. Our ISA-certified arborists can diagnose the problem and recommend a treatment plan that may include pruning infected branches, applying fungicides, improving soil conditions, or adjusting watering practices. The sooner you call, the better the chances of saving the tree.

Yes, we provide free, no-obligation estimates for all tree service work. One of our experienced estimators will visit your property, assess the trees and conditions, and provide a detailed written quote that covers the complete job. There is never any pressure or commitment required. Call (870) 555-0147 or fill out our online contact form to schedule yours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, personal checks, and all major credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Payment is due upon completion of the work. For larger projects, we can discuss payment arrangements during the estimate process. We do not require deposits or upfront payments for standard residential jobs.

How can I tell if a tree on my property is dangerous?

Warning signs of a hazardous tree include large dead branches in the canopy, visible cracks or splits in the trunk, mushrooms or fungal growth at the base, a noticeable lean that has developed recently, exposed or heaving roots, and cavities or hollow areas in the trunk. If you notice any of these signs, contact us for a professional tree health assessment to determine the level of risk.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover tree removal?

Many homeowner's insurance policies cover tree removal if the tree has fallen due to a covered event such as a storm, lightning strike, or wind damage, especially if it has damaged a structure or is blocking a driveway. Coverage varies by policy, so we recommend contacting your insurance provider. We can provide documentation and photos to support your claim if needed.

Do you handle trees near power lines?

Yes, we have the training and equipment to safely work on trees near power lines. For trees that are in direct contact with utility lines, we coordinate with the local power company (Entergy or the local electric cooperative) to ensure the lines are de-energized or properly protected before work begins. Safety is always our top priority in these situations.

What is an ISA-certified arborist?

An ISA-certified arborist is a tree care professional who has been certified by the International Society of Arboriculture. Certification requires passing a comprehensive exam covering tree biology, diagnosis, maintenance practices, safety, and tree risk assessment. Certified arborists must also complete ongoing continuing education to maintain their credentials. Having ISA-certified arborists on our team means your trees receive expert, science-based care.
