Trumann is a welcoming community of approximately 7,200 residents located in Poinsett County, just 17 miles southeast of Jonesboro along the Highway 63 corridor. Known for its strong sense of community and its deep agricultural roots, Trumann sits in the flat, fertile lowlands between Crowley's Ridge and the St. Francis River — a landscape where trees grow vigorously in rich alluvial soil but face persistent threats from severe weather, high water tables, and the structural problems that come with rapid, unchecked growth.

The residential neighborhoods surrounding the Trumann School District campus and along Main Street feature mature pecans, sweetgums, water oaks, and loblolly pines that have been part of the town's character for decades. Many of these trees were planted when the houses were built and have since grown to impressive size, providing shade and curb appeal but also developing the deadwood, co-dominant stems, and root conflicts that aging trees in heavy clay soils commonly exhibit. Regular structural pruning by an ISA-certified arborist is the most cost-effective way to manage these issues and extend the productive lifespan of valuable shade trees.

Trumann's proximity to the St. Francis River and the surrounding agricultural drainage ditches means many properties deal with seasonal moisture that keeps soils saturated for weeks at a time during spring. Trees growing in these conditions often develop shallow, spreading root systems rather than deep taproots, making them far more susceptible to windthrow when the powerful thunderstorms and occasional tornadoes that characterize the spring severe weather season move through Poinsett County. Our ISA-certified arborists evaluate root stability and soil conditions as part of every tree health assessment, identifying at-risk trees before they fail.

The Highway 63 corridor that connects Trumann to Jonesboro is a vital commercial strip where businesses depend on well-maintained lots and professional landscaping. We provide commercial tree care including hazard removal, structural trimming, lot clearing for new development, and ongoing maintenance programs. For Trumann homeowners, we offer the same comprehensive menu of services — tree removal, trimming, stump grinding, emergency storm response, and health assessments — with ISA-certified expertise, transparent pricing, and complete cleanup on every job.
