Dependable Tree Care for Forrest City and St. Francis County

Forrest City is the county seat of St. Francis County, located in the heart of the east Arkansas Delta with a population of approximately 14,000 residents. The city sits along Interstate 40, roughly midway between Little Rock and Memphis, and serves as the commercial hub for a largely agricultural region. With the St. Francis National Forest — the only national forest in the Mississippi Delta — just to the east, Forrest City is surrounded by some of the most densely wooded bottomland in the state.

The trees in and around Forrest City reflect the Delta's rich alluvial soils and abundant moisture. The St. Francis River basin supports extensive stands of bottomland hardwoods including water oak, willow oak, bald cypress, tupelo gum, sweetgum, and pecan. In town, mature shade trees line the older residential streets near the courthouse square and along Washington Street, while the neighborhoods off Crowley's Ridge Road and the areas near East Arkansas Community College feature a mix of planted ornamentals and native species.

St. Francis County is one of the most storm-prone areas in Arkansas. The flat Delta terrain offers virtually no wind break, and severe thunderstorms moving through the region can produce destructive straight-line winds exceeding 70 miles per hour, along with tornadoes and heavy hail. These storms regularly cause significant tree damage — snapping trunks, uprooting root balls from saturated soils, and sending large limbs crashing into roofs, vehicles, and power lines. After every major storm event, the demand for emergency tree service in the Forrest City area surges.

Beyond storm response, property owners throughout St. Francis County rely on routine tree maintenance to prevent problems before they start. Regular trimming reduces the risk of storm damage by removing deadwood and reducing canopy weight. Stump grinding eliminates tripping hazards and reclaims yard space. And for landowners with rural acreage, land clearing services help prepare sites for new structures, fencing, or agricultural use. Jonesboro Tree Pros brings the equipment, expertise, and dependability that Forrest City residents deserve.
