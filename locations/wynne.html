Trusted Tree Care for Wynne and Cross County

Wynne is the county seat of Cross County and a tight-knit community of approximately 8,000 residents located about 35 miles south of Jonesboro along Highway 1. The city sits at the southern edge of Crowley's Ridge, where the elevated terrain meets the flat Delta farmland, creating a transitional landscape that supports an unusually diverse mix of tree species. Southern red oak, loblolly pine, sweet gum, pecan, and tulip poplar all grow readily in this area, giving Wynne some of the most varied tree canopy in eastern Arkansas.

Delta Tree Doctors provides comprehensive tree service to Wynne residents and businesses. Our proximity means fast response times, whether you are scheduling routine maintenance or dealing with storm damage that requires immediate attention. Our ISA-certified arborists understand the specific growth habits and vulnerabilities of the trees common to Cross County, including the aggressive root spread of water oaks in clay soil and the susceptibility of mature pines to ice loading during winter storms.

From the tree-lined residential streets near the Cross County Courthouse to commercial properties along Highways 1 and 64, our crews handle jobs of every scale. We work with homeowners managing a single problem tree, property managers maintaining multi-unit complexes, and landowners clearing acreage for agricultural or construction use. Every project includes a free on-site estimate, transparent pricing, and thorough debris cleanup when the work is done.

Storm Preparedness and Seasonal Tree Care in Wynne

Cross County experiences a full range of severe weather throughout the year, from intense spring thunderstorms and occasional tornadoes to damaging ice events in winter. The diverse tree canopy in Wynne means that storm damage takes many forms. Pine trees are vulnerable to ice loading that can snap trunks and split crowns, while the broad-leafed hardwoods catch wind like sails and are more prone to uprooting in saturated soil. Our emergency crews respond to Wynne around the clock and can typically arrive within a few hours of a call, even during widespread storm events when demand is highest.

Beyond emergency response, we strongly recommend seasonal maintenance to reduce storm risk before severe weather arrives. Late winter is an ideal time for pruning dead and weakened limbs, thinning dense canopies to reduce wind resistance, and removing trees that have developed structural problems. Our arborists can evaluate your property, prioritize the trees that pose the greatest risk, and develop a maintenance plan that fits your budget. Proactive tree care is consistently less expensive than emergency removal, and it protects both your property and your family's safety during Arkansas's unpredictable storm season.
