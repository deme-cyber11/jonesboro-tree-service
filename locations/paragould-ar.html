Trusted Tree Care for Paragould and Greene County

Paragould is the county seat of Greene County and one of northeast Arkansas's most charming communities, with a population of approximately 29,000 residents. Nestled along Crowley's Ridge — a unique geological formation that runs north to south through the eastern half of the state — Paragould enjoys a diverse landscape of rolling terrain, rich hardwood forests, and fertile lowlands that set it apart from the flat Delta farmland just to the east.

The trees that thrive along Crowley's Ridge are notably different from those found in much of the surrounding region. White oaks, tulip poplars, beeches, and hickories flourish in the well-drained soils of the ridge, while sweetgum, sycamore, and bald cypress dominate in the lower-lying areas near drainage creeks and ditches. This botanical diversity is one of the things that makes Paragould a beautiful place to live, but it also means property owners face a wide range of tree care challenges throughout the year.

Whether you own a historic home near downtown Paragould along Pruett Street, a newer subdivision off Highway 412 on the west side of town, or rural acreage out past Greene County Tech, our crew at Jonesboro Tree Pros understands the specific species, soil conditions, and weather patterns that affect your trees. We travel Highway 412 from Jonesboro regularly and know Greene County well.
