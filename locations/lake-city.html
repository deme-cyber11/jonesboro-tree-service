Tree Service in Lake City, Arkansas

Just 12 miles from our Jonesboro base — fast response times for Craighead County tree care.

Lake City is a tight-knit community of approximately 2,500 residents located in the eastern portion of Craighead County, just 12 miles east of Jonesboro along Highway 18. Despite its small size, Lake City has a distinct identity shaped by its proximity to the Cache River National Wildlife Refuge and the surrounding agricultural lowlands that define the landscape of eastern Craighead County. The town's residential streets are lined with mature shade trees — pecans, water oaks, sweetgums, and loblolly pines — that homeowners value for their beauty, shade, and property value but that also require regular professional maintenance to keep healthy and safe.

The Cache River wetlands that border the Lake City area are recognized as a wetland of international importance under the Ramsar Convention, supporting diverse bottomland hardwood forests of bald cypress, tupelo, overcup oak, and willow. While these protected forests are managed by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, the influence of the wetland ecosystem extends into the residential and agricultural properties around Lake City. Trees growing in the heavy clay and alluvial soils near the Cache River drainage often develop shallow root systems that make them vulnerable to windthrow during severe thunderstorms, a persistent risk in this part of Northeast Arkansas.

Lake City's close proximity to Jonesboro means our crew can be on site faster than almost any other community in our service area. For emergency calls involving fallen trees on structures, driveways, or power lines, that response time advantage can make a significant difference in limiting damage and restoring safety. We maintain 24/7 availability and deploy fully equipped crane trucks, bucket trucks, and commercial chippers to handle storm events of any size across the Lake City area.

The Lake City Municipal Airport area and the residential neighborhoods along Main Street and surrounding blocks feature a mix of established homes with large yard trees and newer construction on former agricultural parcels. Whether you need a single dead pine removed, seasonal pruning on the pecans shading your back patio, stump grinding after a recent removal, or a full land-clearing project for a new build, Jonesboro Tree Pros brings ISA-certified quality, transparent pricing, and guaranteed cleanup to every Lake City job.
