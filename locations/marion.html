Marion is a fast-growing city in Crittenden County, positioned along the I-55 corridor just minutes north of West Memphis and the Memphis, Tennessee metropolitan area. With a population that has surged past 13,000 in recent years, Marion has become one of the most desirable suburban communities in eastern Arkansas, attracting families drawn to the highly rated Marion School District and the easy commute across the Mississippi River into Memphis. That growth has brought new subdivisions carved from former farmland and wooded tracts, creating an ongoing demand for professional tree care.

The residential neighborhoods throughout Marion feature a mix of newly planted ornamental trees in younger developments and established hardwoods in the older sections of town near downtown and the original Marion Historic District. Water oaks, southern red oaks, pecan trees, and sweetgums are prevalent throughout the community, and the fertile alluvial soils of the Mississippi River delta provide ideal growing conditions that push these species toward rapid canopy expansion. Without regular pruning, large limbs can develop weak attachments and structural defects that become dangerous during the severe thunderstorms and straight-line wind events that frequently sweep through the delta lowlands.

Marion's proximity to the Mississippi River floodplain creates specific tree care challenges that our arborists are trained to handle. Saturated soils can compromise root stability, and species like silver maple and boxelder — common volunteer trees in the area — are especially prone to storm breakage. We also see significant ice storm damage in Crittenden County every few years, which can snap limbs and split trunks across entire neighborhoods overnight. Our 24/7 emergency response team is equipped to handle widespread storm events with crane trucks, bucket trucks, and commercial chippers.

Whether you own a home in one of Marion's newer developments along I-55, manage commercial property near the interchange, or maintain a large lot in the rural areas outside of town, Jonesboro Tree Pros brings ISA-certified expertise and fully insured professionalism to every job.
