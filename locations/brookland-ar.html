Your Neighborhood Tree Service in Brookland

Brookland is one of the fastest-growing communities in Craighead County, with a population of approximately 3,000 residents that continues to climb year after year. Located just 8 miles north of Jonesboro along Highway 49, Brookland has transformed from a quiet rural village into a thriving bedroom community where young families, first-time homebuyers, and established residents alike are attracted by the excellent Brookland School District, affordable new construction, and the small-town atmosphere that sits within easy commuting distance of Jonesboro's jobs and amenities.

That rapid residential growth has created strong demand for land clearing and lot preparation services. New subdivisions are being developed on formerly wooded and agricultural land around Brookland, and each lot that needs to be cleared before construction begins represents a tree service job. Developers break ground on entire neighborhoods that require coordinated clearing of dozens of trees, stumps, and brush piles. Individual homeowners building custom homes on rural parcels need the same work done on a smaller scale. Jonesboro Tree Pros handles both, providing efficient lot clearing that keeps construction timelines on schedule.

For Brookland residents already settled into their homes, tree care needs tend to center on the maintenance of existing landscape trees. Many of the newer subdivisions were built on land that previously had scattered hardwoods — oaks, sweetgums, hackberries, and the occasional walnut or pecan — and builders often preserved a few of these trees during construction to provide shade and character to the new lots. These preserved trees, along with ornamentals and shade trees planted by homeowners after move-in, need regular trimming to maintain their shape, clear rooflines and gutters, and remove deadwood before storm season arrives.

Because Brookland is so close to our Jonesboro base — just a 10-minute drive up Highway 49 — we can offer some of the fastest response times in our entire service area. For emergency calls during storm events, our crew can reach Brookland properties in as little as 15 minutes. For routine scheduled work, our proximity means flexible scheduling and minimal travel overhead, which translates to competitive pricing for Brookland homeowners.
