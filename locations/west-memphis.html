West Memphis is the largest city in Crittenden County and serves as the primary gateway into Arkansas from the Memphis, Tennessee metropolitan area. Situated at the junction of Interstate 40 and Interstate 55, West Memphis is a hub for commerce, logistics, and entertainment — anchored by the Southland Casino Racing complex that draws visitors from across the region. With a population of approximately 25,000, the city supports a mix of commercial corridors, established residential neighborhoods, and industrial zones, all of which have distinct tree care needs that our ISA-certified arborists are equipped to handle.

The Mississippi River floodplain that defines West Memphis creates both lush growing conditions and significant tree care challenges. The deep alluvial soils produce vigorous growth in species like cottonwood, willow oak, green ash, and American sycamore, but those same saturated soils can undermine root stability during periods of heavy rain or seasonal flooding. Trees growing in flood-prone areas near the river levee system and along Eighth Street require regular health assessments to detect root rot, lean development, and structural compromise before they become hazards to nearby structures and power lines.

Commercial properties along the I-40 service road and Broadway Avenue corridor depend on well-maintained landscaping to support their businesses. We provide commercial tree trimming, hazard removal, parking lot clearing, and ongoing maintenance programs tailored to the needs of West Memphis business owners. For the residential neighborhoods south of Broadway and in the areas surrounding the West Memphis School District campuses, we offer tree trimming, removal, stump grinding, and emergency storm response services with the same ISA-certified quality and transparent pricing that has made us a trusted name across Northeast Arkansas.

West Memphis sits in one of the most storm-active regions of the state, with severe thunderstorms, occasional tornadoes, and winter ice events that can devastate urban tree canopies. Our 24/7 emergency response team deploys crane trucks, bucket trucks, and industrial chippers to clear storm damage quickly and safely.
