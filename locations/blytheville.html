Professional Tree Care for Blytheville

Blytheville is the county seat of Mississippi County and a historic community in the far northeastern corner of Arkansas. Situated approximately 65 miles northeast of Jonesboro along Interstate 55, Blytheville sits in the heart of the Arkansas Delta where the flat, fertile landscape supports large cottonwood, pecan, water oak, and bald cypress trees. These species grow quickly in the region's rich alluvial soil and humid conditions, creating both beautiful canopy cover and ongoing maintenance demands.

Jonesboro Tree Pros serves Blytheville property owners with the full range of professional tree services. From removing hazardous deadwood in the older neighborhoods near Main Street to clearing lots for agricultural or commercial development along the I-55 corridor, our crews arrive with commercial-grade equipment and the expertise to handle any job safely and efficiently. Mississippi County's flat terrain and occasional severe weather, including high winds, ice storms, and thunderstorms, make regular tree inspection and maintenance critical for property protection.

Our arborists are familiar with the specific challenges Delta trees face, including root system instability in saturated soil, rapid regrowth that threatens power lines and structures, and the fungal and pest issues that thrive in high-humidity environments. Whether you need a single tree trimmed or a multi-acre parcel cleared, we provide honest assessments, fair pricing, and thorough cleanup on every job in Blytheville.

Common Tree Issues in the Blytheville Area

Mississippi County's alluvial soil retains significant moisture, which promotes rapid tree growth but also creates conditions where root systems can become shallow and unstable. Large cottonwoods and silver maples are especially susceptible to wind throw during thunderstorms, and pecan trees in the area frequently develop dead interior wood that can drop without warning. Our crews use professional rigging and crane-assisted removal techniques when necessary to safely handle these situations, particularly in tight residential lots where fallen limbs could damage homes, fences, or vehicles.

The humid Delta climate also creates a favorable environment for fungal diseases and wood-boring insects that can compromise tree health from the inside out. Hypoxylon canker, oak wilt, and emerald ash borer are all concerns in this region. Our ISA-certified arborists can identify early signs of disease or infestation during a comprehensive tree health assessment and recommend treatment options that may save the tree, or advise removal when the risk to surrounding property is too great. Scheduling regular inspections is one of the most cost-effective ways Blytheville property owners can protect both their trees and their investment.
