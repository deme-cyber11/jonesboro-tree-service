Paragould is the county seat of Greene County and the second-largest city in the Northeast Arkansas region, situated just 22 miles north of Jonesboro along the Highway 49 corridor. With a population of approximately 30,000, Paragould blends small-town character with steady residential growth, and its tree-lined streets are a defining feature of the community. From the mature oaks shading Linwood Park to the towering pecans in the neighborhoods around the Paragould Community Center, this city has a deeply rooted urban canopy that requires professional care to stay healthy and safe.

The downtown historic district along Pruett Street features some of the oldest trees in the city, many of them planted during Paragould's early twentieth-century growth as a railroad hub. These heritage trees add charm and property value, but they also demand specialized pruning to manage deadwood, structural weakness, and disease. Our ISA-certified arborists understand the specific needs of mature hardwoods common to the Crowley's Ridge area, including white oak, post oak, shagbark hickory, and black walnut.

Paragould's agricultural setting means many properties on the edge of town sit on former farmland where fast-growing volunteer trees like hackberry, silver maple, and eastern red cedar have established themselves over the past few decades. These species grow quickly but often develop structural problems that make them prone to splitting during the severe thunderstorms and ice events that regularly affect Greene County. Proactive trimming and periodic health assessments can prevent storm damage before it happens, saving homeowners thousands of dollars in emergency removal costs.

The Highway 49 commercial corridor that connects Paragould to Jonesboro is lined with businesses that depend on well-maintained landscaping to attract customers. We provide commercial tree care including structural pruning, hazard tree removal, and lot clearing for new construction along this vital economic stretch. Whether your property is on a quiet residential street near Greene County Tech or along a busy commercial strip, Delta Tree Doctors delivers the same ISA-certified quality and complete cleanup on every Paragould job.
