Serving Northeast Arkansas

Based in Jonesboro, our crews travel throughout the region to provide tree removal, trimming, stump grinding, emergency service, health assessments, and land clearing. Whether you are in Craighead County or a neighboring community, we are ready to help.

Delta Tree Doctors operates from our home base in Craighead County and serves communities across a wide swath of northeast Arkansas. Our primary service radius extends approximately 100 miles from Jonesboro, covering major population centers along the I-555 corridor, Highway 49, Highway 1, and Interstate 55. This area encompasses a remarkably diverse landscape, from the hardwood-covered ridges of Crowley's Ridge to the flat, fertile Delta bottomlands along the Mississippi River floodplain.

Each community we serve presents its own unique tree care challenges. The elevated terrain of Crowley's Ridge supports dense stands of white oak, tulip poplar, and beech that are uncommon elsewhere in the Delta. Meanwhile, communities in the river lowlands deal with fast-growing cottonwoods, water oaks, and bald cypress that thrive in wet soil but are prone to root instability during storms. Our ISA-certified arborists have extensive experience with both ecosystems and tailor their approach to match the specific conditions of each property we visit.

We maintain the same standards of professionalism regardless of distance. Every job, whether in downtown Jonesboro or a rural property near Searcy, includes a free on-site estimate, transparent written pricing, full debris cleanup, and the same insured, licensed crew working to ANSI A300 pruning standards. Our 24/7 emergency service is available throughout the entire service area, ensuring that storm damage and hazardous tree situations are addressed quickly no matter where you are in the region.

In addition to the cities listed below, we regularly serve smaller communities throughout the region including Brookland, Lake City, Trumann, Marion, Batesville, Forrest City, Bay, Bono, Caraway, Marked Tree, and Nettleton. Rural properties and acreage between our named service cities are also within our range. Our land clearing crews frequently work on agricultural parcels and new construction sites in the counties surrounding Jonesboro, and our emergency teams respond to storm damage calls across the entire region without regard to municipal boundaries.
