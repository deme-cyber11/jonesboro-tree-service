Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about tree service in Jonesboro, AR.

How much does tree removal cost in Jonesboro, AR?

Tree removal costs in Jonesboro typically range from $300 to $3,000 depending on the tree's size, location, and condition. Small trees (under 30 feet) generally cost $300 to $800, while large trees (60+ feet) can range from $1,500 to $3,000 or more. We provide free on-site estimates so you know the exact cost before any work begins.

Do you offer emergency tree service?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency tree service throughout Jonesboro and Craighead County. Whether a storm has brought down a tree on your home, a large limb is blocking your driveway, or a damaged tree poses an immediate safety hazard, our crew can respond quickly to secure the situation and remove the danger.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Jonesboro Tree Pros is fully licensed and insured in the state of Arkansas. We carry comprehensive general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage, protecting both our crew and your property. Our team includes ISA-certified arborists who maintain ongoing professional education.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Jonesboro and the greater Craighead County area, including Paragould, Batesville, Searcy, West Memphis, Forrest City, Trumann, Brookland, and Lake City. If you're within our service radius and need tree care, give us a call for a free estimate.
