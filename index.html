What Waiting Costs

Every season a hazardous or overgrown tree goes unaddressed, the risk compounds:

  • Dead limbs become heavier and more brittle. One windstorm during tornado season and it's no longer a question of if but where it lands.
  • Trees near structures shift root systems as they grow — what's 10 feet from your foundation today can be 6 feet from it in two years, with roots probing the slab.
  • Emergency removal runs 30–50% more than a planned job. Add property damage and you've paid three to five times what a proactive trim would have cost.
  • Homeowner's insurance: If an insurer determines the hazard was "known and neglected," coverage can be reduced or denied. That determination starts with whether the tree was visibly dead or diseased before it fell.

The longer a hazardous tree stands, the worse the math gets.
