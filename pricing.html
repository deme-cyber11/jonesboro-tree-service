What Affects Tree Service Pricing?

Every tree and property is different. Here are the main factors that influence what you'll pay.

Tree Size & Height

The single biggest factor. A 20-foot ornamental tree is a very different job from a 70-foot oak. Taller trees require more equipment, more crew time, and more safety rigging.

Location on Property

Trees near power lines, structures, fences, or other landscaping cost more to service because they require precision cuts, rigging, and often a crane to control how debris falls.

Tree Species & Condition

Hardwoods like oak and hickory are denser and heavier than softwoods like pine. Dead or diseased trees can be structurally unpredictable, requiring extra precautions.

Urgency & Timing

Emergency calls, after-hours response, and storm damage situations typically cost more than scheduled service due to the urgency and conditions involved.

Accessibility

If our equipment can't reach the tree easily — tight backyards, hillsides, or properties with no truck access — the job takes longer and may require specialized rigging.

Scope of Work

A simple trim is different from a full removal with stump grinding. Bundling services (e.g., removal + stump grinding) often saves money compared to booking them separately.
