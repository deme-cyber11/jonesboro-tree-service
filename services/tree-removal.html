When Does a Tree Need to Be Removed?

Not every problem tree needs to come down. Our arborists will always give you an honest assessment before recommending removal. However, there are clear signs that removal is the safest option:

  • More than 50% of the canopy is dead or dying -- once a tree loses the majority of its crown, recovery is unlikely and the risk of falling limbs increases significantly.
  • Trunk damage or decay -- large cracks, cavities, or hollow sections in the main trunk compromise the tree's structural integrity.
  • Root system failure -- heaving soil, exposed roots, or fungal growth at the base can indicate root rot, which makes the entire tree unstable.
  • Severe lean toward a structure -- a tree that has developed a new lean (not one it grew into naturally) may be on the verge of falling.
  • Storm damage beyond repair -- split trunks, torn-off crowns, and major limb loss from ice storms or tornadoes often make removal the only practical choice.
  • Disease or pest infestation -- certain conditions like oak wilt, emerald ash borer, or advanced bacterial leaf scorch cannot be reversed and may spread to nearby trees.

If you are unsure whether your tree needs removal or trimming, we offer tree health assessments to give you a clear diagnosis and recommendation.

Tree Removal Pricing in Jonesboro

Tree removal costs in Jonesboro typically range from $400 to $2,500 depending on several factors:

  • Small trees (under 30 feet): $400 - $700. Includes ornamental trees, small pines, and younger hardwoods.
  • Medium trees (30-60 feet): $700 - $1,500. Common for mature sweetgums, elms, and mid-size oaks found in most Jonesboro neighborhoods.
  • Large trees (over 60 feet): $1,200 - $2,500. Includes tall pines, large oaks, and old-growth hardwoods, especially those near structures or power lines.

Factors that affect the final price include the tree's proximity to your home, power lines, or fences; the complexity of access (backyard vs. front yard); and whether the trunk is hollow or structurally compromised. We provide free on-site estimates with no obligation -- what we quote is what you pay.

Our Removal Process

Safety is the foundation of every removal we perform. Here is how a typical tree removal job works with Delta Tree Doctors:

  1. Free consultation and estimate -- we visit your property, evaluate the tree, and provide a written quote on the spot or within 24 hours.
  2. Job planning -- our crew lead develops a removal plan that accounts for drop zones, rigging points, neighboring structures, and utility lines.
  3. Safety zone setup -- we establish a clear work zone with cones and rope barriers to keep people and pets safe during the operation.
  4. Sectional dismantling -- starting from the top, we remove limbs and trunk sections in controlled pieces, lowering them with ropes when necessary.
  5. Trunk felling or sectioning -- the main trunk is either felled directionally into an open area or cut in sections if space is limited.
  6. Cleanup and haul-away -- every branch, log, and wood chip is cleared from your property. We leave your yard cleaner than we found it.
