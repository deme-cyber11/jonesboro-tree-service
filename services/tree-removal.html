Trusted Tree Removal Service in Jonesboro, Arkansas

When a tree on your property becomes a liability rather than an asset, Jonesboro Tree Pros delivers safe, thorough tree removal you can count on. Whether you are dealing with a dead oak threatening your roof, a storm-damaged pine leaning over your driveway, or a healthy tree that simply needs to go for a new construction project, our crew has the training, equipment, and local experience to get the job done right. Our tree removal services typically range from $500 to $2,500 depending on the size, species, location, and condition of the tree.

Jonesboro sits in the heart of Craighead County, where the landscape is dotted with mature hardwoods, pines, sweetgums, and other species that thrive in northeast Arkansas's warm, humid climate. These trees add beauty and shade, but they can also grow too large for their surroundings, develop structural weaknesses, or succumb to disease. When that happens, professional removal is the safest and most responsible course of action.

When Is Tree Removal Necessary?

Not every problem tree needs to come down. Our ISA-certified arborists will always explore alternatives like pruning or cabling before recommending removal. However, tree removal becomes the best option when:

  • The tree is dead or dying — Dead trees lose structural integrity quickly and can fall without warning, especially during the severe thunderstorms that regularly move through the Jonesboro area from spring through fall.
  • Significant storm damage has occurred — If a tree has lost more than 50 percent of its canopy or has a split trunk, it usually cannot recover safely.
  • Root systems are damaging foundations or utilities — Large root systems can crack foundations, lift sidewalks, and invade sewer lines in older Jonesboro neighborhoods.
  • The tree is dangerously leaning — A sudden lean, especially after heavy rain when Craighead County's clay soils become saturated, indicates root failure.
  • Disease or insect infestation is beyond treatment — Conditions like oak wilt, pine bark beetle infestations, or severe fungal decay may make removal the only responsible choice.
  • Construction or landscaping projects require it — New builds, additions, pool installations, and fence lines sometimes require tree removal to proceed.

Trees We Remove: Every Size, Every Species

Our crews are equipped and trained to remove every tree species common to northeast Arkansas. This includes large hardwoods such as red oak, white oak, and pecan trees that can reach 80 feet or more, as well as loblolly pines, sweetgum, Bradford pear, maple, elm, ash, and hackberry. We also remove ornamental trees, fruit trees, and overgrown shrub trees like crape myrtle and Eastern red cedar. No tree is too tall, too wide, or too awkwardly positioned for our team.

We specialize in tight-space removals near homes, garages, power lines, and fences. Using precision rigging techniques and sectional cutting, we can safely dismantle trees in confined areas without damaging surrounding structures or landscaping. For exceptionally large or complex removals, we bring in a crane to lift sections over obstacles, keeping everything controlled and predictable.

Our Tree Removal Process

Every tree removal follows a systematic, safety-first approach:

  1. Free On-Site Assessment — We visit your property to evaluate the tree's size, condition, species, and surroundings. We check for power lines, structures, and other obstacles, then provide a detailed written estimate with no hidden fees.
  2. Planning & Preparation — We determine the safest removal method and handle any required permits through the City of Jonesboro. We notify you of the scheduled date and explain exactly what to expect on the day of service.
  3. Safe, Professional Removal — Our crew arrives with the right equipment for the job, whether that means climbing gear for technical removals or a crane for large hazardous trees. We use controlled cutting techniques and rigging to lower limbs safely, protecting your home, landscaping, and neighboring properties.
  4. Complete Cleanup & Hauling — Once the tree is down, we buck the trunk into manageable sections, chip all branches on site, and rake the area clean. All debris is hauled away, and the only thing left behind is a neat stump ready for optional grinding.
  5. Stump Grinding (Optional Add-On) — We offer a bundled discount when you combine tree removal with stump grinding. Our commercial-grade grinder removes the stump six to twelve inches below grade so you can resod or replant the area.

What Affects the Cost of Tree Removal?

Tree removal pricing in Jonesboro typically ranges from $500 to $2,500, depending on several factors. The biggest cost drivers are the height and trunk diameter of the tree, since larger trees require heavier equipment and more labor hours. Location matters as well: a tree standing alone in an open yard costs less to remove than one wedged between a house and a fence line, because tight-access removals require more precise rigging and sectional cutting. Hazardous conditions like proximity to power lines, severe lean, or decay that makes the tree unpredictable also increase the price. We always provide a detailed written estimate before any work begins so there are no surprises on your bill.

Safety Protocols and Insurance

Tree removal is inherently dangerous work, and we treat every job with the seriousness it deserves. Every member of our crew carries current certifications in chainsaw safety and aerial operations. We use industry-standard personal protective equipment on every job site and follow ANSI Z133 safety standards for arboricultural operations. Before any cutting begins, we establish a clear drop zone, identify potential hazards such as overhead power lines or nearby structures, and brief every team member on the removal plan. Our $2 million liability insurance policy and full workers' compensation coverage protect you and your property throughout the process.

Tree Removal FAQs

Common questions about tree removal services in Jonesboro, AR.

How much does tree removal cost in Jonesboro, AR?

Tree removal in Jonesboro typically costs between $500 and $2,500 depending on the tree's size, species, location, and condition. Small trees under 30 feet generally cost $500 to $800, medium trees from $800 to $1,500, and large trees over 60 feet from $1,500 to $2,500 or more. We provide free on-site estimates so you know the exact cost before work begins.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Jonesboro?

In most cases, you do not need a permit to remove a tree on your own residential property within Jonesboro city limits. However, trees located in easements, near public right-of-ways, or within certain planned developments may require approval. Our team checks permit requirements as part of every estimate so you never have to worry about compliance.

How long does a typical tree removal take?

Most residential tree removals in Jonesboro are completed within a single day. Small to medium trees (under 40 feet) usually take two to four hours, including cleanup. Large hardwoods or technically challenging removals near structures may take a full day. We provide a time estimate along with your price quote.

Is stump grinding included with tree removal?

Stump grinding is offered as an optional add-on service. After removal, we cut the stump as close to ground level as possible. If you want it completely removed, our stump grinder takes it six to twelve inches below grade so you can resod or replant the area. We offer a bundled discount when you schedule both services together.
