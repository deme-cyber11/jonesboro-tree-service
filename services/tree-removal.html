Tree Removal in Jonesboro, AR

When a tree becomes a liability rather than an asset, professional removal is the safest and most responsible course of action. Delta Tree Doctors provides full-service tree removal throughout Jonesboro, Craighead County, and the surrounding communities of Northeast Arkansas. Whether you are dealing with a dead oak leaning toward your roof, a storm-damaged pine threatening power lines, or a diseased sweetgum that has become a hazard to your family, our licensed and insured crews handle every removal with precision, safety equipment, and a commitment to leaving your property cleaner than we found it.

Northeast Arkansas is home to a wide variety of tree species that each present unique removal challenges. The towering post oaks and water oaks that line Jonesboro neighborhoods can exceed 80 feet in height and carry canopies spanning 60 feet or more. Loblolly pines grow tall and straight but become dangerously brittle when weakened by ice storms or southern pine beetle infestations. Sweetgum trees, while common along Crowley's Ridge and throughout Craighead County, develop aggressive root systems that buckle sidewalks and foundations. We have removed every species that grows in this region and understand the structural characteristics, root behavior, and failure patterns that determine how each tree needs to come down safely.

When Tree Removal Is the Right Call

Not every problematic tree needs to be removed. Our ISA-certified arborists will always give you an honest assessment before recommending removal. However, there are situations where removal is the only responsible option:

  • Dead or dying trees — A tree that has lost the majority of its canopy, shows extensive bark separation, or has large fungal growths at the base is structurally compromised and can fall without warning.
  • Storm-damaged trees — Jonesboro and Craighead County experience severe thunderstorms, ice storms, and occasional derecho events that split trunks, snap major limbs, and uproot root plates. Trees that survive a storm with major structural damage rarely recover safely.
  • Leaning trees threatening structures — A tree that has developed a progressive lean toward your home, garage, fence, or power lines presents an immediate risk that worsens over time.
  • Diseased trees beyond treatment — Oak wilt, hypoxylon canker, and root rot can compromise a tree's structural integrity to the point where treatment cannot restore safety.
  • Construction and development — New home construction, additions, pool installations, and driveway expansions sometimes require tree removal. We work with contractors across Jonesboro on residential and commercial projects.

Our Tree Removal Process

Every tree removal we perform follows a structured process designed to protect your property, our crew, and your neighbors. Here is exactly what to expect when you hire Delta Tree Doctors for a removal job.

1

Assessment & Quote

An ISA-certified arborist visits your property, evaluates the tree's size, species, lean, proximity to structures and power lines, and determines the safest removal method. You receive a transparent, written quote the same day — no hidden fees, no pressure.

2

Safe Removal

On removal day, our crew arrives with the appropriate equipment — chainsaws, rigging systems, bucket trucks, and cranes for large or confined removals. We section the tree from top to bottom, using controlled rigging to lower limbs and trunk sections safely. For trees near structures, we use crane-assisted removal to eliminate risk to your roof, fence, or utility lines.

3

Cleanup & Haul-Away

Once the tree is down, we chip all branches, cut the trunk into manageable sections, and haul everything off your property. We rake the area and blow off driveways and walkways. If you want stump grinding as an add-on, we can handle that the same day in most cases.

Equipment for Every Situation

Not all tree removals are straightforward. In Jonesboro, homes are often built close together with mature trees growing within feet of rooflines, fences, and power lines. Our fleet includes 75-ton cranes capable of lifting entire trunk sections over structures, bucket trucks with 75-foot reach for high canopy work, and commercial chippers that process debris on-site. For tight-access properties in older Jonesboro neighborhoods near downtown or along Highland Drive, we use specialized rigging techniques to dismantle trees piece by piece without heavy equipment entering your yard.

Storm Damage and Urgent Removals

Craighead County sits in a region that experiences severe weather from early spring through late fall. Thunderstorms with straight-line winds, ice storms that coat branches in inches of heavy ice, and the occasional tornado all take a toll on the tree canopy across Jonesboro. After a major storm event, our phones light up with calls from homeowners dealing with trees on roofs, across driveways, and tangled in power lines. We offer 24/7 emergency tree removal with priority response times for situations involving structural damage or safety hazards. If a tree is on your house or blocking your driveway right now, call us at (870) 771-3364 — we answer around the clock.

Stump Grinding Add-On

After a tree is removed, the stump remains. We strongly recommend grinding the stump below grade at the time of removal — it saves you money compared to scheduling a separate visit, and it allows you to immediately reclaim that space in your yard. Our stump grinders take stumps down 8 to 12 inches below grade, and we backfill with the resulting wood chips. The area can be seeded with grass or used for new landscaping within weeks.

When You Need Tree Removal in Jonesboro

If you notice any warning signs — dead branches in the canopy, mushrooms or conks growing at the base, bark peeling away from the trunk, a visible lean that seems to be increasing, or roots lifting out of the ground — do not wait for the next storm to make the decision for you. A tree that fails on its own schedule can land on your roof, your car, your fence, or worse. Proactive removal on your terms is always safer, cheaper, and less stressful than emergency removal after failure.
