What Our Land Clearing Service Includes

Land clearing is a comprehensive process that goes well beyond just cutting down trees. Our full-service approach covers every phase of the project:

  • Tree removal and felling -- all standing trees on the designated clearing area are felled, sectioned, and removed. For projects with marketable timber (hardwoods of sufficient size and quality), we can coordinate with local sawmills to offset your costs.
  • Brush and undergrowth removal -- all brush, saplings, vines, and undergrowth are cleared using forestry mulchers and brush cutters. This includes invasive species like privet, honeysuckle, and kudzu that are common in northeast Arkansas.
  • Stump grinding and root extraction -- depending on the intended use of the land, we either grind stumps below grade (suitable for agricultural conversion) or fully extract stumps and root balls (required for construction sites where excavation will follow).
  • Debris hauling and disposal -- all wood, brush, and vegetative debris is either chipped on-site, hauled to an approved disposal facility, or burned on-site where local regulations permit (we handle all burn permits in the Jonesboro area).
  • Rough grading -- after clearing, we can perform basic rough grading to leave the site level and ready for the next phase of construction, farming, or landscaping.
  • Selective clearing -- if you want to preserve certain trees (mature oaks, specimen trees, or trees along property lines for screening), we work around them carefully, protecting root zones and bark from equipment damage.

Common Use Cases for Land Clearing

Our clients in the Jonesboro area request land clearing for a wide range of purposes:

  • Residential construction -- clearing wooded lots for new home construction, including driveway access, building pad preparation, and septic field clearing. This is our most common land clearing request as Jonesboro continues to grow, with new subdivisions expanding into wooded areas along Crowley's Ridge and south of the city.
  • Commercial development -- preparing larger tracts for commercial buildings, parking areas, and infrastructure. We work with general contractors to meet their site preparation timelines and specifications.
  • Agricultural conversion -- converting wooded or overgrown land back to farmable acreage. Craighead County has some of the most productive agricultural land in Arkansas, and clearing projects help landowners put underutilized acreage back into production.
  • Fence line and property line clearing -- removing trees and brush along fence rows and property boundaries for new fence installation, survey access, or property maintenance.
  • Recreational property -- clearing trails, food plots, camping areas, and access roads on rural recreational properties. We can create clearings while preserving the natural character of the surrounding woods.
  • Overgrown property reclamation -- restoring neglected residential or commercial properties that have become overgrown with volunteer trees, brush, and invasive species.

Land Clearing Pricing in Jonesboro

Land clearing costs in the Jonesboro area range from $1,000 to $10,000 depending on the scope and complexity of the project:

  • Small residential lots (under 1/2 acre): $1,000 - $3,000. Typical for single home lots with light to moderate tree density. Includes tree removal, brush clearing, stump grinding, and debris hauling.
  • Large residential lots (1/2 to 2 acres): $3,000 - $6,000. Common for rural home sites with heavier tree cover. May include selective clearing to preserve desirable trees.
  • Multi-acre clearing (2-5+ acres): $4,000 - $10,000+. Agricultural conversion, commercial sites, and large development projects. Price depends heavily on tree density, terrain, and whether stump extraction vs. grinding is required.

Several factors affect land clearing costs: the density and size of trees, the type of terrain, accessibility for heavy equipment, whether stumps need full extraction or just grinding, debris disposal method, and whether any trees have marketable timber value. We provide free on-site estimates for all land clearing projects and can typically start within one to two weeks of approval.

Equipment and Capabilities

Efficient land clearing requires the right equipment for the job. Jonesboro Tree Pros maintains a full fleet of commercial land clearing equipment:

  • Forestry mulchers -- track-mounted mulching heads that can process standing trees, brush, and stumps in a single pass. Ideal for clearing dense undergrowth and small-diameter trees quickly.
  • Excavators with grapple attachments -- for lifting and stacking logs, pulling stumps, and rough grading after clearing.
  • Commercial chippers -- high-capacity chippers that process branches and small logs into wood chips on-site, reducing the number of debris hauling trips.
  • Stump grinders -- both compact and full-size units for below-grade stump removal across the entire cleared area.
  • Chainsaws and felling equipment -- for precision felling of large trees, especially near property lines, utilities, or structures that must be protected.
  • Skid steers with brush cutter and bucket attachments -- versatile machines for clearing, grading, and material handling on smaller lots and tight-access sites.

Timber Handling and Salvage

If your property has marketable timber -- mature hardwoods like oak, walnut, or hickory of sufficient diameter and quality -- we can coordinate timber salvage as part of the clearing process. In some cases, timber value can offset a meaningful portion of the clearing cost. We work with local mills in the northeast Arkansas region and can advise you on whether your timber has salvage value during our initial site walk.

For trees without timber value, we process everything on-site through chipping and mulching. The resulting wood chips can be spread on the cleared site as ground cover, used for erosion control, or hauled away as part of the project scope.
