Professional Land Clearing in Jonesboro, Arkansas

Whether you are preparing a lot for new construction, clearing fence lines for a ranching operation, reclaiming an overgrown property, or opening up acreage for agricultural use, Jonesboro Tree Pros has the heavy equipment, experienced operators, and local knowledge to handle land clearing projects of any scale throughout Jonesboro and Craighead County.

Northeast Arkansas has seen significant growth in recent years, and Jonesboro's expanding development means more property owners need reliable land clearing services. Our team works with homeowners building their dream house on a wooded lot, contractors preparing commercial sites, farmers converting timber land to cropland, and property owners dealing with neglected parcels that have become overgrown with brush, vines, and volunteer trees. We understand the specific terrain, soil conditions, and regulations in the Jonesboro area, and we bring that local expertise to every project.

Types of Land Clearing Projects We Handle

Our land clearing services cover a wide range of residential and commercial applications:

  • Residential Lot Clearing — Preparing individual home sites for new construction. We remove trees, brush, and stumps to give your builder a clean, level work surface. We can selectively clear around trees you want to keep for shade and landscaping.
  • Commercial Site Preparation — Clearing land for commercial buildings, parking lots, retail developments, and industrial facilities. We work with general contractors and developers to meet project timelines and specifications.
  • Fence Line Clearing — Removing trees, brush, and undergrowth along existing or planned fence lines for agricultural properties, ranches, and rural homesites. Clean fence rows make installation easier and reduce maintenance.
  • Overgrown Property Reclamation — Restoring neglected lots and properties that have become overgrown with invasive species, volunteer trees, and thick brush. Common in Jonesboro neighborhoods where vacant lots have been untended for years.
  • Right-of-Way Clearing — Clearing vegetation along utility easements, access roads, and drainage ditches for municipalities, utility companies, and property management firms.
  • Agricultural Conversion — Converting wooded or brushy acreage to productive farmland. We remove all vegetation and stumps, leaving the land ready for grading, tilling, and planting.

Our Equipment and Capabilities

Effective land clearing requires the right equipment for the job. Our fleet includes:

  • Commercial Chainsaws and Rigging Gear — For precision tree felling and removal in areas where heavy equipment cannot access or where selective clearing is needed.
  • Skid Steer with Forestry Mulching Head — Our forestry mulcher grinds standing brush, small trees (up to 6 inches in diameter), and undergrowth into fine mulch in a single pass. This is the most efficient method for clearing dense brush and small-diameter vegetation.
  • Excavator with Grapple — For removing large trees, root balls, and heavy debris from the work site. The grapple attachment allows precise handling of logs and brush piles.
  • Commercial Stump Grinder — Every stump left after tree removal is ground below grade so the land is truly clear and ready for its next use.
  • Brush Chipper — Our commercial chipper processes branches and brush on-site, reducing volume by 10 to 1 for efficient disposal or on-site mulch use.
  • Dump Trucks — For hauling away logs, brush, and debris when the material cannot be processed on-site.

Our Land Clearing Process

Every land clearing project follows a systematic process from initial assessment to final completion:

  1. Site Assessment & Estimate — We walk the property with you to understand your goals, identify trees to keep or remove, note any utilities or easements, and assess the terrain. We then provide a detailed written estimate that covers all aspects of the clearing work.
  2. Planning & Permitting — We research all applicable permits and regulations, including floodplain restrictions, wetland protections, and city ordinances. We coordinate with Arkansas 811 to mark underground utilities before any equipment enters the site.
  3. Tree and Brush Removal — Our crew removes all designated trees, starting with the largest timber and working down to brush and undergrowth. Trees designated to remain are protected with physical barriers and root zone buffers.
  4. Stump Grinding — Every stump is ground six to twelve inches below grade, ensuring a smooth surface free of obstacles for construction equipment, grading, or planting.
  5. Debris Removal & Site Cleanup — All logs, brush, and debris are chipped, hauled, or processed on-site according to the plan. We leave the cleared area clean and ready for the next phase of your project.

Selective Clearing: Keep the Trees You Love

Not every land clearing project requires removing every tree on the property. Many homeowners want to preserve select shade trees, specimen oaks, or mature hardwoods that add character and value to their land. Our arborists work with you to identify which trees are healthy, structurally sound, and worth keeping. We then mark those trees clearly and take precautions during the clearing process to protect their root zones, bark, and branches from equipment damage. This selective approach gives you the best of both worlds: a cleared, buildable site with the natural beauty of established trees.

Land Clearing FAQs

Common questions about land clearing services in Jonesboro, AR.

How much does land clearing cost in Jonesboro, AR?

Land clearing costs in Jonesboro vary widely based on the size of the area, density of vegetation, number and size of trees, and accessibility. Small residential lots with light brush may start around $1,500, while larger properties with heavy timber can range from $3,000 to $10,000 or more per acre. We provide free on-site estimates for every project.

Do I need a permit for land clearing in Jonesboro?

Permit requirements depend on the location and scope of the clearing project. Most residential clearing within Jonesboro city limits does not require a specific clearing permit, but projects in floodplains, wetlands, or near waterways may require permits from the Army Corps of Engineers or Arkansas DEQ. We research all permit requirements as part of our planning process.

Can you clear land without damaging trees I want to keep?

Absolutely. Selective clearing is one of our specialties. Before any work begins, we walk the property with you and clearly mark which trees to keep and which to remove. Our operators are experienced in working around designated save trees, and we take precautions to protect their root zones from equipment damage.

What happens to the cleared material?

We offer several options for cleared material. Trees can be chipped on-site for mulch, cut into firewood-length logs for your use, or hauled away entirely. Brush and small vegetation is typically chipped on-site. Stumps are ground below grade. We work with you to determine the best approach for your specific project and goals.
