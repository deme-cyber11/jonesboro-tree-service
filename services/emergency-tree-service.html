24/7 Emergency Tree Service in Jonesboro, Arkansas

When a severe storm tears through Craighead County and drops a massive oak across your driveway at 2 AM, you need a tree service that answers the phone and responds fast. Jonesboro Tree Pros provides around-the-clock emergency tree service for homeowners and businesses throughout Jonesboro and the surrounding communities. Our emergency crews are equipped, trained, and ready to handle the most dangerous tree situations at any hour of the day or night.

Northeast Arkansas is no stranger to severe weather. Thunderstorms, tornadoes, straight-line winds, and ice storms can strike with little warning, turning healthy trees into immediate hazards. When the damage is done, the priority is getting the danger removed safely and quickly so you can begin recovering. That is exactly what our emergency team is built to do.

When to Call for Emergency Tree Service

Not every tree issue qualifies as an emergency, but when any of the following situations occur, you should call us immediately:

  • A tree has fallen on your home or structure — This is the most urgent situation. A fallen tree can compromise your roof's integrity, create openings for water damage, and pose ongoing collapse risks.
  • A tree is blocking your driveway or road — Fallen trees across roadways create dangerous obstructions, especially at night. We work with local authorities to clear access as quickly as possible.
  • A tree or large limb is on power lines — Never approach a downed tree that is touching power lines. Call your utility company first, then call us. We coordinate with Entergy Arkansas for safe removal near energized lines.
  • A partially fallen tree is hanging over your home — A tree that has partially uprooted or split but has not fully fallen is extremely dangerous. It can complete its fall at any moment, especially with additional wind or rain.
  • A large limb is dangling overhead — Broken branches caught in the canopy, known as widow-makers, can drop without warning. If a hanging limb is over a walkway, play area, or parking spot, it needs immediate attention.

Our Emergency Response Process

When you call our emergency line, here is what happens:

  1. Immediate Phone Assessment — We gather critical information about the situation over the phone: what happened, is anyone injured, are power lines involved, and what structures are affected. This helps us dispatch the right crew with the right equipment.
  2. Rapid Dispatch — For emergencies within Jonesboro and Craighead County, our crews typically arrive on-site within two to four hours, depending on conditions. During major storm events, we triage calls based on severity and respond to the most dangerous situations first.
  3. Scene Safety Assessment — Before any cutting begins, our crew leader evaluates the scene for hazards including downed power lines, structural damage, gas leaks, and unstable trees. We establish a safe work zone and communicate the plan to you.
  4. Hazard Removal — We use controlled cutting techniques, rigging, and heavy equipment as needed to safely remove the fallen tree or dangerous limb. Our goal is to eliminate the immediate danger and secure your property.
  5. Debris Cleanup — Once the hazard is cleared, we remove all debris from the work area. If further work is needed, such as stump grinding or additional trimming, we schedule a follow-up visit at standard rates.

What to Do During a Tree Emergency

If a tree emergency occurs on your property, follow these steps to stay safe while you wait for our crew:

  • Evacuate if necessary. If a tree has fallen on your home and you can see structural damage, get everyone out of the house and to a safe location.
  • Stay away from downed power lines. Assume all downed lines are energized. Keep a distance of at least 35 feet and call Entergy Arkansas at their emergency line.
  • Do not attempt to remove the tree yourself. Storm-damaged trees are under enormous tension and can shift, roll, or spring without warning. Leave removal to trained professionals with the right equipment.
  • Document the damage. Once it is safe, take photos and video of the damage for your insurance claim. Document the tree's position, any structural damage, and the overall scene.
  • Call Jonesboro Tree Pros at (870) 771-3364. We answer emergency calls around the clock and will get a crew to you as quickly as possible.

Insurance Claims Assistance

Dealing with insurance after storm damage is stressful. We make it easier by providing detailed documentation of the damage, before-and-after photos, and itemized invoices that insurance adjusters need to process your claim. In most cases, homeowners insurance covers emergency tree removal when a tree falls due to a covered event such as wind, lightning, or ice and damages an insured structure. Our office staff is experienced in working with all major insurance carriers and will assist you throughout the claims process.

Storm Season Preparedness

The best way to handle a tree emergency is to prevent one. We recommend scheduling a tree health assessment before storm season to identify weak, dead, or structurally compromised trees on your property. Proactive trimming and removal of hazardous trees is far less expensive and disruptive than dealing with emergency damage after a storm. Call us to schedule a pre-storm inspection and take the worry out of the next severe weather event.

Emergency Tree Service FAQs

Common questions about emergency tree services in Jonesboro, AR.

How fast can you respond to a tree emergency in Jonesboro?

For emergencies within Jonesboro and Craighead County, we typically arrive on-site within two to four hours of your call, depending on conditions. During major storm events, we prioritize calls based on immediate danger to life and property, and we work around the clock until all emergency situations are resolved.

What should I do if a tree falls on my house?

First, ensure everyone in the home is safe and evacuate if there is structural damage. Do not go near the fallen tree, especially if power lines are involved. Call 911 if anyone is injured, then call us at (870) 771-3364. We will dispatch an emergency crew immediately to secure the situation and begin removal.

Does homeowners insurance cover emergency tree removal?

In most cases, homeowners insurance covers tree removal when a tree falls due to a covered event like a storm, wind, or lightning and damages an insured structure. Coverage typically ranges from $500 to $1,000 per tree. We work directly with insurance adjusters and provide all necessary documentation to support your claim.

Do you charge extra for after-hours emergency calls?

Emergency service rates are higher than standard service rates due to the urgency, risk, and after-hours labor involved. However, we are always transparent about pricing and will provide a cost estimate before beginning any work, even in emergency situations. We never take advantage of a crisis to overcharge.
