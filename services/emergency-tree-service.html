Emergency Tree Service in Jonesboro, AR

When a tree crashes through your roof at 2 a.m. during a thunderstorm, you need a crew that answers the phone and shows up ready to work. Delta Tree Doctors provides 24/7 emergency tree service throughout Jonesboro, Craighead County, and all of Northeast Arkansas. Our emergency crews are equipped, insured, and trained to handle fallen trees on homes, vehicles, driveways, power lines, and any urgent hazard that cannot wait until morning. We answer our phones around the clock, 365 days a year — because trees do not wait for business hours to fail.

Northeast Arkansas sits in a region that experiences some of the most severe weather in the country. The convergence of warm Gulf moisture and cold fronts from the plains creates a storm pattern that delivers intense thunderstorms, derechos, ice storms, and tornadoes to Craighead County and the surrounding communities with regularity. Every spring, powerful thunderstorm cells move through Jonesboro producing straight-line winds that exceed 70 miles per hour, snapping trunks, uprooting root plates, and sending massive limbs crashing into homes and across roadways. Winter brings ice storms that coat branches in heavy ice, turning every mature tree on your property into a potential hazard as limbs bow and break under the accumulated weight.

Common Emergency Scenarios We Handle

Our emergency crews respond to a wide range of urgent tree situations across Jonesboro and the surrounding area. Here are the most common calls we receive:

  • Tree on house or roof — This is the most urgent call we receive. A tree resting on your roof can cause progressive structural damage with every passing hour, especially during rain. We prioritize these calls above all others and work to stabilize the situation as quickly as possible.
  • Tree blocking driveway or road — After a storm, fallen trees across driveways, private roads, and parking lots prevent residents from leaving their property or accessing their homes. We clear these obstructions rapidly so you can get back to normal.
  • Tree on vehicle — A large limb or entire tree on your car or truck is both a safety hazard and an insurance matter. We remove the tree carefully to minimize additional vehicle damage and provide photo documentation for your insurance claim.
  • Tree on power lines — Fallen trees tangled in power lines are extremely dangerous. Do not approach. Call us and your utility provider immediately. We coordinate with Craighead Electric Cooperative, Entergy Arkansas, and the City of Jonesboro utilities to safely de-energize lines before removal work begins.
  • Partially uprooted or leaning trees — A tree that has partially uprooted or developed a sudden lean during a storm may not have fallen yet, but it can come down at any moment. These situations require immediate professional assessment to determine whether the tree can be stabilized or needs immediate removal.
  • Storm-split trunks — Severe storms can split a tree trunk down the middle, leaving two halves leaning in opposite directions. This is structurally catastrophic and the tree needs to come down before either half falls uncontrolled.

What to Do When a Tree Falls

If a tree has fallen on or near your home during a storm, follow these steps to stay safe while you wait for our crew to arrive:

  • Evacuate if necessary — If a tree has compromised the structural integrity of your home or if you smell gas, leave the building immediately and call 911.
  • Stay away from downed power lines — Assume any wire on the ground is live. Keep at least 35 feet of distance and keep children and pets away.
  • Do not attempt to remove the tree yourself — Chainsaws and fallen trees are an extremely dangerous combination, especially in storm conditions with wet footing and tangled debris. Leave it to trained professionals.
  • Document the damage — If it is safe to do so, take photographs from a safe distance for your insurance claim.
  • Call us at (870) 771-3364 — We will get a crew to you as quickly as possible.

Insurance Documentation and Claims Support

Dealing with your insurance company after storm damage is stressful. We make it easier by providing detailed photo documentation of the damage, written descriptions of the work performed, and itemized invoices that insurance adjusters can process quickly. Most homeowner insurance policies in Arkansas cover tree removal when a tree has fallen on an insured structure (your home, garage, shed, or fence). We have worked with every major insurance carrier in the region and can help you understand what your policy is likely to cover before work begins.
