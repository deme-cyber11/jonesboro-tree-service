When to Call for Emergency Tree Service

Not every tree situation requires an emergency response. However, the following scenarios warrant an immediate call to our 24/7 line at (870) 771-3364:

  • A tree has fallen on your home, garage, or vehicle -- even a partial impact can cause structural damage and create openings for water intrusion. We work alongside your insurance adjuster to document the damage and remove the tree safely.
  • A tree is blocking your driveway or road -- fallen trees that prevent access to your property or obstruct public roadways need to be cleared as soon as possible for safety and accessibility.
  • A tree has fallen on power lines -- this is an extremely dangerous situation. Stay at least 40 feet away, call your utility provider first, and then call us. We coordinate with Craighead Electric and Entergy to remove trees from energized lines safely.
  • A tree is actively splitting or leaning -- if a tree is cracking, splitting at the trunk, or has developed a sudden lean after a storm, it could fall at any moment. Do not wait until it comes down on its own.
  • Large hanging branches (hangers) -- broken limbs that are caught in the canopy and could drop at any time pose a serious risk to anyone underneath.
  • Uprooted trees leaning on structures -- a tree that has partially uprooted but is resting against a building or fence is unstable and can shift without warning.

Our Emergency Response Process

When you call our emergency line, here is what happens:

  1. Immediate phone response -- a real person answers your call, day or night. We do not use answering services or voicemail for emergencies.
  2. Rapid dispatch -- we dispatch a crew to your location as quickly as possible. For most emergencies within Jonesboro, we can be on-site within 1-3 hours depending on storm conditions and current call volume.
  3. Safety assessment -- our crew lead evaluates the scene for hazards including downed power lines, gas leaks, and structural instability before any work begins.
  4. Stabilization -- if the tree is resting on a structure, we stabilize the situation to prevent further damage while we develop a removal plan.
  5. Safe removal -- using rigging, cranes, and sectional cutting techniques, we remove the tree from the structure or roadway without causing additional damage.
  6. Documentation -- we photograph the scene before and during removal to support your insurance claim. We can also work directly with your adjuster.
  7. Complete cleanup -- all debris is removed from your property. We leave the site clean and safe.

Storm Damage in Jonesboro: What You Need to Know

Jonesboro sits in a region that experiences frequent severe weather events. Understanding storm damage risks can help you prepare:

  • Tornado season (March-June) -- northeast Arkansas falls within Tornado Alley. High winds can snap trunks, uproot entire root plates, and launch debris into canopies.
  • Ice storms (December-February) -- ice accumulation is one of the most destructive forces for trees. Even a quarter inch of ice can bring down large limbs, and heavier accumulations can topple entire trees.
  • Summer thunderstorms -- straight-line winds from summer storms regularly exceed 60 mph in the Jonesboro area, causing widespread limb breakage and occasional tree failures.
  • Saturated soil -- heavy rain events soften the soil, reducing root anchoring. Trees that are already compromised by decay or root damage are most vulnerable during wet conditions.

The best defense against storm damage is proactive tree care. Regular trimming and health assessments can identify and address weak points before a storm turns them into emergencies.
