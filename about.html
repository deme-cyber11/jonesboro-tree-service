About Jonesboro Tree Pros

Locally owned and operated since 2009, providing expert tree care to Jonesboro and Craighead County with ISA-certified arborists on staff.

Our Story

Jonesboro Tree Pros was founded in 2009 with a simple mission: to provide the residents and businesses of Jonesboro, Arkansas with honest, professional tree care they could count on. What started as a two-person crew with a single truck and a chainsaw has grown into one of Craighead County's most trusted tree service companies, employing a team of skilled technicians and an ISA-Certified Arborist who oversees every major project.

Over the past fifteen years, we have removed, trimmed, and treated thousands of trees across northeast Arkansas. We have weathered ice storms, tornado seasons, and brutal summer heat waves alongside our neighbors, responding quickly when they needed us most. Our deep roots in the Jonesboro community are not just a figure of speech — our founder grew up right here in Craighead County, and most of our crew members call this area home.

From the beginning, we chose to invest in proper training, commercial-grade equipment, and full insurance coverage because we believe that the people of Jonesboro deserve the same quality of tree care you would find in any major city. That commitment has earned us a 4.9-star rating across 136 reviews and the trust of homeowners, property managers, and municipalities throughout the region.

As a locally owned and operated business, we understand the unique challenges that Jonesboro's climate and soil conditions present for tree health. Our humid subtropical weather, with hot summers and occasional ice storms in winter, creates specific stressors that out-of-state companies simply do not appreciate. We know which native species are most vulnerable to disease, which invasive pests to watch for, and how to time pruning and treatment for the best results in our region.

We are fully licensed in the state of Arkansas and carry comprehensive general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage. This protects our crew, your property, and your peace of mind. We also provide free, no-obligation estimates on every job, so you always know exactly what the work will cost before we begin. There are no hidden fees, no surprise charges, and no pressure to add services you do not need.

Our team takes pride in leaving every job site cleaner than we found it. All debris, wood chips, brush, and logs are removed unless you ask us to leave the firewood. We respect your landscaping, your driveway, and your schedule. When we say we will be there at 8 AM, we are there at 8 AM. That kind of reliability, combined with ISA-certified expertise and transparent pricing, is what has made Jonesboro Tree Pros the go-to tree service for homeowners and businesses across Craighead County for more than fifteen years.

Why Choose Jonesboro Tree Pros

Four reasons homeowners and businesses across Craighead County trust us with their trees.

ISA-Certified Arborists

Our lead arborist holds International Society of Arboriculture certification, bringing science-based expertise to every project. From diagnosing tree diseases to planning safe removals, ISA certification means your trees receive the highest standard of professional care available in northeast Arkansas.

Fully Licensed & Insured

We carry comprehensive general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage, fully licensed in the state of Arkansas. Your property and our crew are protected on every job. We are happy to provide proof of insurance upon request before any work begins.

Local Jonesboro Expertise

We live and work in Craighead County. We know Arkansas soil conditions, weather patterns, native tree species, and regional pests. This local knowledge allows us to provide targeted care that generic national companies cannot match, keeping your trees healthy year-round.

Free, Honest Estimates

Every project starts with a free on-site estimate. We walk your property, assess the work, and provide a detailed written quote with no hidden fees or surprise charges. You will know the exact cost before any work begins, and we never pressure you into services you do not need.

Our Values

Everything we do at Jonesboro Tree Pros is guided by a set of core values that have defined our company since day one.

  • Safety First Every crew member is trained in OSHA safety standards. We use proper rigging, protective equipment, and follow strict protocols on every job to protect people and property.
  • Honest Communication We tell you what your trees actually need, not what generates the biggest invoice. If a tree can be saved with treatment instead of removal, we will recommend the more affordable option.
  • Respect for Your Property We protect your lawn, landscaping, and driveway during every job. Our crews use ground mats, careful rigging, and thorough cleanup to leave your property looking better than before.
  • Community Commitment We are proud to be part of the Jonesboro community. Our team lives here, shops here, and raises families here. We are invested in keeping Craighead County's urban forest healthy for generations to come.
