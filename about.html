About Delta Tree Doctors

12 years of professional tree care for Jonesboro, Craighead County, and the surrounding communities of Northeast Arkansas.

Rooted in Jonesboro. Committed to Every Tree.

Delta Tree Doctors was founded over 12 years ago with a simple mission: provide honest, professional tree care to the people of Northeast Arkansas. What started as a small crew with a single chipper has grown into a full-service tree company trusted by homeowners, property managers, and commercial clients across Craighead County.

Our team includes ISA-certified arborists who understand the unique growing conditions of the Crowley's Ridge region. From the deep-rooted hardwoods along the ridge to the lowland species in the delta flats, we know the trees that make this area home — and we know how to care for them properly.

Local Knowledge, Professional Standards

Northeast Arkansas presents challenges that out-of-town contractors simply do not understand. Ice storms, severe spring weather, and the clay-heavy soils of Craighead County all impact tree health and stability in ways that require local expertise. Our arborists have spent years working in these conditions, and that experience shows in every job we take on.

We are fully licensed to operate in Arkansas and carry comprehensive general liability and workers' compensation insurance. Every member of our crew is trained to OSHA safety standards, and we follow ANSI A300 pruning specifications on every trim job.

Our Promise

We believe in transparent pricing, honest assessments, and leaving your property cleaner than we found it. If a tree can be saved with proper pruning or treatment, we will tell you. If removal is the safest option, we will explain exactly why. No upselling, no pressure — just professional guidance you can trust.
