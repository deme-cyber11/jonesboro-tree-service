About Jonesboro Tree Pros

12 years of trusted tree care in Jonesboro, AR and across Northeast Arkansas.

Our Story

Jonesboro Tree Pros was founded over 12 years ago by a pair of Jonesboro locals who grew up climbing the oaks and pines along Crowley's Ridge. What started as a two-person crew with a single truck and a chainsaw has grown into one of Northeast Arkansas's most trusted tree service operations.

We didn't grow by cutting corners. We grew because we showed up on time, gave honest quotes, did clean work, and treated every customer's property like our own. That's still how we operate today. Every job, every time.

From our first residential trimming job in 2014 to the large-scale commercial clearing projects we handle today, our commitment hasn't changed: deliver professional-grade tree care at fair, transparent prices, backed by real expertise and real accountability.

ISA-Certified Arborists on Every Job

Our team holds certifications from the International Society of Arboriculture (ISA), the gold standard in tree care. That means every job gets expert-level diagnosis, planning, and execution — not guesswork.

ISA Certified

Our arborists hold active ISA certifications and stay current through continuing education. We know Arkansas tree species inside and out — their diseases, their growth patterns, and how to care for them properly.

Licensed & Fully Insured

We carry comprehensive general liability and workers' compensation insurance on every project. You're protected from the first phone call to the final cleanup. Proof of insurance is available upon request.

Professional Equipment

We operate a full fleet of cranes, bucket trucks, chippers, and commercial stump grinders. The right equipment means safer, faster, and cleaner results for every job.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every job we take on.

Honesty

No hidden fees, no upselling, no pressure. We give you a straightforward assessment of what your trees need — and what they don't. If trimming will solve the problem, we won't push removal.

Safety

Tree work is inherently dangerous. We follow OSHA and ANSI Z133 safety standards on every job site. Our crew is trained, our equipment is inspected, and your property is protected by full insurance coverage.

⭐

Quality

We don't do half-jobs. Every project gets ISA-certified oversight, proper technique, complete debris removal, and a final walkthrough. We leave your property cleaner than we found it.
