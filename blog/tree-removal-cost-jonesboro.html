How Much Does Tree Removal Cost in Jonesboro, AR?

A comprehensive 2025 pricing guide for homeowners in Jonesboro and Craighead County.

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Tree Removal Cost in Jonesboro
February 10, 2025 8 min read Tree Removal

If you have a tree on your property that needs to come down, one of your first questions is probably "How much is this going to cost me?" It is a fair question, and the answer depends on several factors specific to your situation. In this guide, we break down the typical tree removal costs in Jonesboro, AR for 2025 so you can budget accordingly and know what to expect when you request a quote.

Average Tree Removal Costs by Size

The single biggest factor in tree removal pricing is the size of the tree. Taller trees require more labor, specialized equipment, and carry greater risk during the removal process. Here is what homeowners in Jonesboro can expect to pay in 2025:

Tree Size Height Typical Cost Range
Small Under 30 feet $300 – $500
Medium 30 – 60 feet $500 – $1,000
Large 60 – 80 feet $1,000 – $2,500
Very Large 80+ feet $2,500 – $4,500+

These ranges reflect pricing typical to the Jonesboro and Craighead County market. Costs in northeast Arkansas tend to be somewhat lower than national averages due to the region's lower cost of living, but the specific characteristics of your tree and property can push prices higher or lower.

Factors That Affect Tree Removal Cost

While tree size sets the baseline, several other factors influence what you will actually pay. Understanding these helps you anticipate your quote and ask the right questions when a tree service visits your property.

1. Tree Location and Accessibility

A tree standing in the middle of an open yard is straightforward to remove. A tree wedged between your house, your fence, and a power line is a completely different job. Difficult access adds cost because the crew must use specialized rigging techniques, work more slowly, and sometimes bring in crane equipment to lower sections safely. If a tree is near Entergy power lines, the utility company may need to be involved, which can add time to the process.

2. Tree Condition

Dead, diseased, or structurally compromised trees are unpredictable. Branches may break unexpectedly, the trunk may be hollow or weakened, and the wood may be more brittle. This increased risk means the crew needs to take extra precautions, which adds labor time and cost. Conversely, a healthy tree with solid wood is more predictable and often faster to remove.

3. Trunk Diameter

Two trees of the same height can have very different trunk diameters. A thick-trunked oak takes considerably more time to section and remove than a slender pine of the same height. Larger diameter means more cutting, more debris, and more hauling.

4. Number of Trees

If you need multiple trees removed, most companies offer a discount on the per-tree rate. The crew is already on-site with their equipment, so the setup cost is absorbed across multiple removals. If you have two or three trees that need attention, getting them done at the same time will save you money compared to scheduling separate visits.

5. Stump Removal

Tree removal quotes typically cover cutting the tree down and hauling away the debris, but stump grinding is usually an additional charge. In Jonesboro, stump grinding typically costs $100 to $400 per stump, depending on diameter and root system. Many homeowners choose to include stump grinding in their tree removal project to avoid the eyesore and tripping hazard of a remaining stump.

6. Emergency vs. Scheduled Removal

If a tree has fallen on your roof during a storm at 2 AM, that is an emergency, and emergency tree service carries a premium. You can expect to pay 25% to 50% more for emergency removal compared to a scheduled job. The urgency, after-hours labor, and immediate mobilization of crew and equipment all contribute to the higher cost. When possible, scheduling non-urgent removals during regular business hours will save you money.

7. Season and Timing

In Jonesboro, tree service companies tend to be busiest in spring (after storm season) and summer. If your removal is not urgent, scheduling during the late fall or winter months may get you a better rate, as demand is typically lower and crews have more availability.

What Should Be Included in Your Quote

When you receive a tree removal estimate, make sure it covers all the essentials. A thorough quote from a reputable company should include:

  • Felling or sectional dismantling of the tree
  • Limb and trunk removal from your property
  • Debris cleanup and hauling
  • Stump grinding (if included, or quoted separately)
  • Proof of insurance — liability and workers' compensation
  • Estimated timeline for completion

Be cautious of any quote that seems vague or does not specify exactly what work will be performed. A legitimate tree service should be happy to walk you through every line item.

Why the Cheapest Quote Is Not Always the Best

It is tempting to go with the lowest bid, but tree removal is one area where cutting corners can be genuinely dangerous and expensive. Here is why:

  • Insurance matters. An uninsured crew that damages your home, fence, or a neighbor's property leaves you holding the bill. Verify that any company you hire carries general liability insurance and workers' compensation. If a worker is injured on your property and the company has no workers' comp, you could be liable.
  • Experience prevents damage. An experienced crew knows how to drop a tree away from structures, how to rig limbs over obstacles, and how to handle unexpected situations like a hollow trunk or a hang-up. Inexperienced operators are more likely to cause property damage.
  • Proper equipment matters. Professional tree removal often requires bucket trucks, cranes, or specialized rigging. A crew showing up with just a chainsaw and a pickup truck may not have the equipment needed to do the job safely.
  • Cleanup and hauling. Some low-bid operators will cut the tree down but leave the mess for you to deal with. Make sure your quote includes full cleanup and debris removal.

Tips for Getting the Best Value

You do not have to overpay for quality tree removal. Here are practical tips for getting a fair price in Jonesboro:

  1. Get at least three quotes. This gives you a realistic range for your specific job and helps you identify outliers, both high and low.
  2. Ask about bundling. If you have multiple trees or also need trimming work done, ask about package pricing.
  3. Schedule in the off-season. Late fall and winter are typically slower for tree companies, and you may be able to negotiate a better rate.
  4. Verify credentials. Ask for proof of insurance, check online reviews, and confirm the company is licensed in Arkansas.
  5. Get everything in writing. A written estimate protects both you and the tree service company. It should detail the scope of work, total cost, payment terms, and timeline.
