How Much Does Tree Removal Cost in Jonesboro, AR?

2026 pricing guide with real local estimates from ISA-certified arborists.

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. How Much Does Tree Removal Cost in Jonesboro AR?

If you have a dead, damaged, or dangerous tree on your property in Jonesboro, the first question on your mind is probably: how much is this going to cost? The answer depends on several factors, but for most residential tree removals in the Jonesboro, Arkansas area, you can expect to pay somewhere between $400 and $2,500. That is a wide range, so let us break it down by tree size, species, and the specific conditions that drive costs up or down in Craighead County.

Tree Removal Cost by Size

The single biggest factor in tree removal pricing is the height and trunk diameter of the tree. Here is what Jonesboro homeowners typically pay in 2026:

Small Trees: $400 to $700

Small trees are generally under 30 feet tall with trunk diameters under 12 inches. Common small trees in the Jonesboro area include ornamental pears (Bradford pears are everywhere in Northeast Arkansas neighborhoods like Indian Hills and Valley View), crepe myrtles, and young sweetgums. These jobs are relatively straightforward. A two-person crew can usually take down a small tree, cut it into manageable sections, and haul away the debris in two to four hours. If the tree is in an open area with easy access, you will likely land on the lower end of this range.

Medium Trees: $700 to $1,200

Medium trees range from 30 to 60 feet tall and include many of the species that define the Jonesboro landscape: red oaks, water oaks, loblolly pines, and pecan trees. These trees require more equipment, more time, and more expertise. The crew will often need to climb the tree or use a bucket truck, and rigging may be necessary to lower heavy limbs safely, especially if the tree is close to a house, power lines, or a fence. Most medium tree removals in Jonesboro take a half-day to a full day depending on complexity.

Large Trees: $1,200 to $2,500

Large trees over 60 feet tall are the most complex and expensive to remove. In the Jonesboro area, this category includes mature white oaks, post oaks, sycamores, and the towering loblolly pines that line Crowley's Ridge. Some of these trees have trunk diameters exceeding 24 inches and canopy spreads of 40 feet or more. Removing a tree this size requires a crane in some situations, especially if the tree is in a tight backyard, near a structure, or tangled with utility lines. Large tree removals are typically full-day jobs that may extend into a second day for cleanup.

Factors That Affect Tree Removal Cost in Jonesboro

Beyond size, several other variables influence the final price you will pay for tree removal in the Jonesboro area:

Tree Species

Hardwoods like oaks and pecans are significantly heavier and denser than softwoods like pine. A 50-foot white oak weighs considerably more than a 50-foot loblolly pine, which means more labor, more equipment, and more time to process. Hardwood removals typically cost 10 to 20 percent more than softwood removals of the same height. Sweetgum trees, which are extremely common throughout Craighead County, fall in the middle: they are not as dense as oak but their wood is stringy and difficult to cut cleanly, which can add time to the job.

Location and Accessibility

A tree standing in the middle of an open lot is far easier and cheaper to remove than one wedged between your house and your neighbor's fence. If a crane or specialized rigging is needed to avoid damage to structures, pools, sheds, or landscaping, that adds to the cost. In older Jonesboro neighborhoods like the Highland Drive area or near downtown, lots tend to be smaller and more densely built, which can make access more challenging. Properties along Nettleton Avenue or out toward Brookland with larger lots typically see lower costs for the same size tree simply because access is easier.

Stump Grinding

Most tree removal quotes do not include stump grinding unless you specifically ask. Stump grinding is a separate service that typically costs between $150 and $400 per stump in Jonesboro, depending on the stump's diameter and root spread. We recommend removing the stump at the same time as the tree to save on mobilization costs. If you bundle stump grinding with your tree removal, you will often get a better overall price than scheduling them separately.

Emergency Removal Surcharge

When a tree falls on your house during a spring thunderstorm or an ice storm knocks a massive pine onto your driveway, you need someone there fast. Emergency tree removal in Jonesboro typically costs 25 to 50 percent more than a scheduled removal because it requires immediate crew mobilization, often during evenings, weekends, or severe weather events. Northeast Arkansas sees its share of storms, especially during tornado season from March through June, so emergency calls are common during those months.

Debris Hauling and Cleanup

A reputable tree service includes debris removal and basic cleanup in the quote. Be cautious of lowball bids that leave the wood, branches, and chips in your yard for you to deal with. Our tree removal quotes always include full cleanup and haul-away so there are no surprises.

How Jonesboro Compares to National Averages

The national average for tree removal in 2026 ranges from about $500 to $3,000, with a median around $1,100. Jonesboro falls slightly below the national median, largely because the cost of living and labor rates in Northeast Arkansas are lower than in major metro areas. That said, Jonesboro pricing is comparable to similar-sized cities in the Mid-South region like Paragould, West Memphis, and Batesville. You may see slightly higher prices in the Little Rock metro, where demand and overhead costs are higher.

When Is Tree Removal Worth the Investment?

Tree removal is not an expense anyone looks forward to. But delaying removal of a hazardous tree can cost you far more in the long run. A dead or structurally compromised tree that falls onto your home, vehicle, or a neighbor's property can cause tens of thousands of dollars in damage. In the worst case, it can injure someone. If an arborist has identified structural failure, advanced decay, or a significant lean toward a structure, removal is almost always the right call. The cost of proactive removal is a fraction of what you would pay in property damage, liability claims, and emergency service surcharges.

There are also situations where removal simply makes sense for your property. A tree that is blocking sunlight from your garden, lifting your driveway with its roots, or dropping thousands of sweetgum balls across your yard every fall may be worth removing for quality-of-life reasons, even if it is technically healthy.

How to Get a Fair Quote in Jonesboro

The best way to avoid overpaying for tree removal in Jonesboro is to get multiple estimates from licensed, insured companies. Here is what to look for:

  • On-site estimate: Never accept a quote over the phone. A reputable arborist needs to see the tree, assess its condition, measure its size, and evaluate access before giving you an accurate price.
  • Written quote: Get everything in writing, including scope of work, debris removal, stump grinding (if included), timeline, and total cost.
  • Proof of insurance: Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage. Tree work is dangerous, and you do not want to be liable if an uninsured worker is injured on your property.
  • ISA certification: Look for companies with ISA-certified arborists on staff. This certification demonstrates formal training in tree biology, risk assessment, and proper removal techniques.
  • No pressure: Be wary of companies that pressure you to sign immediately or demand large deposits upfront. A trustworthy company will give you time to compare quotes.
